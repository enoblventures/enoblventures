01 OUR APPROACH

As founders and YC alumni ourselves, we know how personal building a company can be. We want to be the investors we would have wanted: supportive, straightforward, and easy to work with.

01 / UNDERSTANDING

You can be honest with us.

You don’t need to have every answer. We listen, ask thoughtful questions, and bring empathy for the pressure and uncertainty of the early days. Respect and kindness matter, especially when things are difficult.

02 / CLARITY

Respect for your time.

We aim to move quickly, communicate clearly, and be direct about where we stand. We keep our expectations realistic and our conversations grounded in where your company is today.

03 / SUPPORT

A partner in the work.

We bring decades of building and investing experience, and help where we can: thinking through a product decision, making an introduction, or talking through a hard call. You lead your company. We’re here to support you.
