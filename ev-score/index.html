EV SCORE

Proprietary intelligence.
Human conviction.

EV Score is our proprietary predictive system for evaluating early-stage companies. It draws on company, founder, financing, and market information to rank opportunities and guide our diligence.

01 FROM SCORE TO SELECTION

A clearer focus
for deeper diligence.

We focus on the top 25% of companies by EV Score. Our investment team then evaluates the business and technology before deciding whether to invest. A high score is not a guarantee of future performance.

02 THE SIGNALS

A company in context.

The system brings together multiple dimensions of a company, rather than relying on a pitch or one isolated metric.

COMPANY

What is being built?

Business description, maturity, age, industry, geography and operating status.

FOUNDERS

Who is building it?

Founder background, biography, education and the context around the team.

FINANCING

How has it developed?

Funding history, round sequence, prior investors, valuations and time between rounds.

CONTEXT

What surrounds it?

Overlapping sector classifications and the wider market and economic environment.

Signal categories drawn from the system’s documented development. Available information varies by company.

03 THE MODELING FOUNDATION

Built on years of
venture modeling.

Much of a startup’s story lives in words. The modeling pipeline was developed to work with descriptions and biographies alongside structured financing and company data.

01

Turn information into comparable signals.

Data is cleaned and normalized. Natural-language processing and embeddings turn descriptive text into numerical features, while classification models add views of industries and markets.

02

Ask several questions of the same company.

The historical architecture includes models for funding, outcomes, valuation, company classification and related analytical tasks. Stage-specific and ensemble approaches let different models contribute to an evaluation.

03

Test on information the model hasn’t learned from.

The documented pipeline preserves out-of-sample predictions for evaluation and calibration. The purpose is to examine predictive information beyond the observations used for training.

A closer look at the technical lineage +

The development documents describe word embeddings, feature reduction, PCA, PLS, UMAP-related approaches and neural-network autoencoders, alongside classification and regression models. These describe the system’s evolution; individual components can change as the models develop.

04 TESTING THE METHODOLOGY

Predictions recorded
before outcomes were known.

In November 2022, approximately 40,000 companies were scored. Subsequent analysis found that higher-scoring companies were more likely to secure funding or achieve an exit.

The study examined company outcomes, not investment returns. It does not establish future fund performance. We are preparing a fuller account of the study’s methodology and comparison groups.

05 UNCERTAINTY IS PART OF THE WORK

A range of possibilities.
A more informed question.

The valuation methodology was designed around distributions of possible values at a future financing event. That makes uncertainty visible rather than hiding it inside a single estimate.

Valuation modeling is one part of the broader architecture. A modeled financing valuation is not an exit prediction, and a score does not guarantee an outcome.

06 THE INVESTMENT DECISION

Evidence to investigate.
Judgment to act.

Our models help prioritize attention and inform questions. Experienced investors and operators conduct diligence and make every final investment decision.
