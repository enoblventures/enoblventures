OUR APPROACH

How we choose
what to back.

We invest exclusively in Y Combinator companies. Our alumni relationships bring us close to founders early. Proprietary analysis helps us prioritize opportunities. Our partners make the final call.

01 WHY YC

A focused universe.
New opportunities.

Y Combinator brings together successive groups of early-stage companies. Focusing on this ecosystem allows us to build relationships, understand its culture, and evaluate companies within a consistent investment universe.

02 THE INVESTMENT PROCESS

Access opens the door.
Conviction takes work.

A high score is a starting point for investigation. It does not replace diligence or an investment decision.

01 / ACCESS

Get close early.

Our YC alumni understand the fundraising process and build relationships with founders, including before Demo Day. Those relationships help us pursue competitive deals.

02 / SCORE

Rank the opportunities.

Our proprietary EV Score evaluates companies and directs deeper diligence toward the top 25% by score.

03 / DILIGENCE

Examine the business.

Investment professionals and operators assess the founders, product, technology, market, and financing.

04 / SELECT

Make the call.

Our partners decide which companies to back, considering both the individual opportunity and its fit within the portfolio.

03 PORTFOLIO CONSTRUCTION

Investing across
companies and time.

Exceptional companies emerge in different years. We build exposure across YC batches, sectors, and years, reducing dependence on any single company or moment in the market.

04 SECTOR FOCUS

Broad perspective.
Specific expertise.

We focus on sectors where we have a clear investment thesis and the expertise to assess the opportunity. For highly specialized companies, we work selectively with external domain experts.

05 HUMAN JUDGMENT

The models inform.
Our partners decide.

Operating, engineering, and portfolio-management experience shape how we evaluate evidence, challenge assumptions, and decide where to invest.
